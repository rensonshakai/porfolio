About Moonlight Hotel

Comfort, warmth, and generous hospitality at the foot of Mount Kenya.

A Comfortable Home in Nanyuki

Moonlight Hotel is conveniently situated in Nanyuki town, behind the G4S Building next to CMC Motors — making it easy to find and easy to get around from.

Whether you are here for business, travel, or to explore the beautiful Laikipia region, we are ready to welcome you.

We offer clean, comfortable rooms with breakfast included, a restaurant serving meals throughout the day, and a conference/boardroom facility for meetings and events.
